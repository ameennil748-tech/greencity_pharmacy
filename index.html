Once you drop or choose a prescription file and fill out the patient contact details, our chief Registered Pharmacist Ameen Ahamad (PCI Reg No: 49301) personally checks the prescription details. We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) or Pay on Pickup, instant UPI payments (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and direct Bank Account transfers (NEFT/IMPS). You can view store payment coordinates and scan UPI links directly in the shopping cart sidebar during checkout.
